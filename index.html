Questions

Is the output actually usable or just generic AI?

The generator is structured around real freelance proposal patterns (problem → approach → scope → timeline → investment → next step). You always edit. The point is to eliminate the blank page, not to ship unreviewed text.

Do I need a credit card for the free plan?

No. Free is free. One proposal a month is enough to test whether it saves you time on real client work.

Can I use this for Upwork or other platforms?

Yes. Generate a strong first draft, then adapt the tone and length to the platform’s norms. Many freelancers use it for both marketplace bids and direct client proposals.

What about contracts and e-signatures?

v1 focuses on proposals and scopes. Simple contract language can be included in the generated document. Native e-sign and payment collection are on the roadmap once core usage is validated.

Is my data used to train models?

No. Client details and proposal content are not used for model training. Privacy policy is straightforward.
