Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 27, 2026

ForgeProp (“we”, “us”) respects your privacy. This policy describes what we collect and how we use it.

Information we collect

When you use the free or paid product we may collect account information (email, name), proposal content you enter, usage metrics, and basic technical data (browser, IP for security and rate limiting).

How we use it

To provide the service, improve the product, process payments (via Stripe), and communicate with you about the product. We do not sell your data. We do not use your proposal content or client details to train third-party models.

Storage & security

Data is stored on reputable cloud providers with industry-standard protections. Free-tier generation currently runs client-side or on limited server processing; paid plans will store history under your account.

Cookies & analytics

We may use essential cookies and privacy-friendly analytics. No invasive tracking for advertising.

Your rights

You may request access, correction, or deletion of your account data by contacting us. You can export or delete proposal history once accounts are enabled.
